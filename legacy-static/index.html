Ask us anything
What is recruitment process outsourcing (RPO)?

RPO means handing some or all of your recruitment function to a specialist partner. infiniti acts as an extension of your HR team, managing sourcing, screening, interviews and onboarding using your brand and tools.

How is this different from a staffing agency?

Agencies typically charge a fee per placement and work on your roles alongside many other clients. With RPO, a dedicated team is embedded in your process, aligned to your goals and accountable for results across your whole hiring plan.

How quickly can you start?

Most engagements kick off within a week of the intake call. For urgent roles, we can begin sourcing the same week while the wider plan is finalised.

Can we outsource only part of our hiring?

Yes. You can choose full-cycle RPO, project-based hiring, on-demand recruiters, or specific stages such as sourcing and screening, and change the model as your needs evolve.

Will candidates know they're talking to infiniti?

That's up to you. Our recruiters can represent your company directly, using your brand, email domains and tone of voice, so candidates experience one consistent team.
